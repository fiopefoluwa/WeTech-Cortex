COMMERCIAL CREATOR & BRAND PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Commercial Creator Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of September 1, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

BRAND:
Nescafe Coffee Nigeria Ltd. ("Brand")
Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria
Represented by: Campaign Partnerships Team

CREATOR:
Amaka Moyinlola ("Creator")
Lagos, Nigeria
Handle: @amaka_creates

WHEREAS, the Brand desires to engage the Creator to produce original social video assets, and the Creator agrees to provide such services under the terms and conditions set forth herein:

SECTION 1: SCOPE OF WORK
1.1 The Creator agrees to produce, edit, and publish high-quality digital creator content highlighting the Brand's seasonal cold brew product line.
1.2 The primary aesthetic shall reflect an authentic lifestyle format aligned with the Creator's established social audience.

SECTION 2: DELIVERABLES & FORMAT SPECIFICATIONS
2.1 Deliverables: Exactly 3 TikTok videos (60 to 90 seconds in duration, vertical 9:16 format, 1080x1920 resolution).
2.2 Timeline:
    - Deliverable #01: Delivery on or before September 15, 2026.
    - Deliverable #02: Delivery on or before September 25, 2026.
    - Deliverable #03: Delivery on or before October 5, 2026.
2.3 Platforms: Primary distribution on TikTok with cross-sharing permitted to Instagram Reels.

SECTION 3: COMPENSATION & MILESTONE DISBURSEMENTS
3.1 Total Fee: The Brand shall pay the Creator a total fixed compensation of ₦300,000 NGN (Three Hundred Thousand Nigerian Naira).
3.2 Disbursement Schedule:
    - Milestone 1: ₦150,000 NGN (50% upfront commitment deposit upon signing).
    - Milestone 2: ₦150,000 NGN (50% upon final delivery and approval of all 3 deliverables).
3.3 Payment Terms: Net-7 days following milestone approval via verified escrow or bank transfer.

SECTION 4: REVISION POLICY & OUT-OF-SCOPE ADJUSTMENTS
4.1 Included Revisions: 1 complimentary round of editorial revision is included per deliverable, provided feedback is delivered within 48 hours of draft receipt.
4.2 Additional Revisions: Subsequent rounds of revision requested by the Brand shall be billed at ₦35,000 NGN per round via an authorized Change Request.
4.3 Out-of-Scope Requests: Any additional formats, extra cutdowns (such as YouTube Shorts, 15-second cutdowns, or podcast clips) not enumerated in Section 2.1 constitute scope changes requiring mutually agreed additional compensation.

SECTION 5: COMMERCIAL LICENSING & USAGE RIGHTS
5.1 Organic Usage: The Creator grants the Brand non-exclusive, worldwide organic usage rights on TikTok and Instagram for a duration of 30 days commencing on the date of initial publication.
5.2 Paid Advertising Restrictions: Paid advertising rights (including Meta Dark Posts, TikTok Spark Ads, or programmatic whitelisting) are NOT included in baseline terms.
5.3 Commercial License Extensions: Paid advertising usage requires an active commercial licensing extension at ₦120,000 NGN per 30-day window.

SECTION 6: EXCLUSIVITY & TERRITORY
6.1 Category Exclusivity: The Creator agrees not to promote direct commercial competitors in the ready-to-drink coffee and beverage sector for the active 30-day term.
6.2 Territory: Worldwide distribution rights across specified social platforms.

SECTION 7: SIGNATURES & EXECUTION
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Commercial Creator Agreement as of the Effective Date written above.
